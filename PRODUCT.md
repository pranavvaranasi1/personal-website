# PRODUCT.md — pranavtech.me

## Register
**brand** — this is a personal portfolio. Design IS the product.

## Who
Pranav Varanasi. Northwestern MBAi student (Kellogg + McCormick). Co-founder of Variant Labs (TechBio AI). Incoming Google AI PM in People Operations (Summer 2026). Ex-Deloitte AI strategy. Builder of three apps (Variant Labs, Adapt, Medicast). Writer on Substack about AI x humanity. Background actor (*Don't Look Up*). Basketball coach. National Parks photographer. Goes to temple. Wears an Om necklace.

## Audience
Tech recruiters, hiring managers at top tech firms (Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta), MBA peers, people who land here from his LinkedIn or writing. Sophisticated taste. Will spend 30 seconds to 3 minutes. The first 5 seconds decide whether they keep going or close the tab.

## Product Purpose
A personal site that does three jobs at once:
1. **Land the job / opportunity** — make recruiters and founders want to talk to him
2. **Showcase AI fluency** — the site itself proves he understands AI as a builder, not a poseur
3. **Tell his story** — the through-line connecting Deloitte strategy, AI engineering, fiction writing, temple-going, basketball coaching, acting

## Thesis
**The site embodies the tension Pranav writes about: AI vs. what is human.**

Every Substack post he's written is about this line — mental health AI, chatbots that say "I'm here for you," hearts that develop minds, going to temple in the age of AI companions. The site doesn't just *talk* about that tension. It *is* that tension.

This thesis governs every design choice. When in doubt, ask: does this honor the human side, or does this honor the machine side, and is the tension visible?

## Strategic Principles

1. **Editorial, not HUD.** Treat this like a New Yorker profile that ships with code, not a sci-fi dashboard. Confident typography, photography, generous whitespace. The "tech feel" comes from behavior (animation, interaction, the digital twin) not chrome (glow, gradient, glass).

2. **Three modes, one site.** Human mode (default cinematic). Agent mode (clean structured text for AI crawlers — and meta-flex). Studio mode (peek behind the curtain — system prompts, data, machinery shown openly). Toggle is visible, owned, conceptually load-bearing.

3. **The avatar is the navigation, optionally.** A floating animated character (stylized version of Pranav — curly hair, Om necklace) walks visitors through the site. Always-on companion in human mode. Becomes the chat surface in Ask mode. Disappears in Agent / Studio.

4. **Each section gets its own motion language.** Don't fade-up everything. The temple essay assembles meditatively. Variant Labs unfolds technically. Acting kicks in cinematic letterboxing. Basketball is scrappy, off-grid. Motion serves narrative, not decoration.

5. **Specificity beats generality.** Real names (Brandeis, Kellogg, McCormick, Deloitte, Google, Variant Labs). Real numbers ($145M, 10K views, 5 engineers managed). Real places. Real photographs. The opposite of "passionate technologist."

6. **Every section earns its scroll.** No filler. Each viewport has one strong idea.

## Anti-References (what this site must NOT look like)

- AI bro Tony Stark HUD — dark + neon blue + glass + grid + floating particles. The first-order cliché.
- Generic dev portfolio — sidebar nav, project cards in a 3-up grid, "Skills: React, TypeScript, Python" pill list.
- Notion-clone editorial — toggle widgets, monospace everywhere, "I'm a curious generalist" cosplay.
- Crypto-deck — neon gradients, animated grid floors, slogan typography ("Build the future").
- Award-show portfolio that's all motion and no content — sites that win Awwwards but you can't tell what the person does.

## Voice (copy throughout site)

Texting a smart friend. Specific, warm, unpretentious. Self-aware about being on the internet in 2026. Examples:

- ✓ "I built the MVP from scratch over 6 months and three almost-quit moments."
- ✗ "I am a passionate technologist passionate about AI."
- ✓ "I write about AI and the temple. Sometimes those are the same essay."
- ✗ "Exploring the intersection of technology and spirituality."

No em dashes. No hedge words ("just", "really"). No "passionate." No "leveraging." Strong verbs. Periods.

## Surfaces (pages)

- `/` — Home. Hero + scroll journey through identity (who → what → where → why).
- `/projects` — Variant Labs, Adapt, Medicast. Each gets a non-template treatment — varied layout, real screenshots, real tech detail.
- `/writing` — Full text of Substack essays embedded inline. Not link cards. Reader view with thoughtful typography.
- `/travel` — 3D globe of places visited. Hover for context. Eventually photographs.
- `/ask` — Digital twin chat. Avatar is the interface. Casual tone, like texting Pranav.
- `/studio` (or accessible via mode toggle) — Behind the curtain. Site source map. The system prompt. Honest about what's AI and what's not.

## What success looks like
A recruiter at Google, Anthropic, or a top startup spends 90+ seconds on the site, reads at least one essay, sends Pranav a message starting with "I loved your site." Someone shares it on Twitter unprompted. Other MBAi students text him asking who built it.
